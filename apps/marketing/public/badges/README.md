# Store badges

Drop the **official** App Store and Google Play badge SVGs here. Filenames must be:

- `app-store.svg` — "Download on the App Store" (Apple)
- `google-play.svg` — "Get it on Google Play" (Google)

## Why official badges only

Both Apple and Google **require** download links to use their unmodified, official
badges (no custom buttons, no recoloring, no emoji equivalents). Using non-compliant
buttons risks store-listing rejection and trademark complaints.

## Where to download

- **Apple App Store badge** — https://tools.applemediaservices.com/app-store/
  - Pick "Download on the App Store"
  - Language: English (US)
  - Format: SVG
- **Google Play badge** — https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/
  - Pick "Get it on Google Play"
  - Language: English
  - Format: SVG (PNG fallback also OK)

## Sizing

The page renders both badges at `height: 54px`, width auto. The Apple badge is
~160px wide and the Google Play badge is ~180px wide at that height — sizing
matches each store's recommended minimums.
